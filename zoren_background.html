📘 6. Qualitative Risk Assessment Demystified: Expressing Qualitative Factors in Cents of Upside or Discount to Recovery Replacing Abstract % Multipliers with Direct, Transparent Cents Attribution per Dollar of Claim

Under classical asset coverage math, clean recovery is 100.0c. Yet, the 2026 notes trade at ~94.5c. Rather than using obscure percentage multipliers (e.g. 15% friction), institutional credit desks demand direct attribution: "How many cents per dollar does each specific risk or catalyst move my bond?" By expressing qualitative risk factors directly as Cents of Upside (+cents) or Discount (-cents) to Recovery, the bridge between theoretical asset coverage and market pricing is transparent and direct: Desk Fair Value = Fundamental Senior Recovery + ∑(Δ Cents).
