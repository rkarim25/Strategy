1. Creditor-on-Creditor Violence vs Bank Syndicate Cooperation: The Game Theory

While Zorlu's fundamental asset coverage is 190.6% ($2,100M operational EV + $240M cash vs $1,200M OpCo loans and $582M HoldCo claims), offshore Eurobond holders ($300M ZOREN 9.00% 2026) are structurally subordinated to project finance banks (EBRD, IFC, Garanti BBVA, Akbank, İşbank). Rather than abstract percentage haircuts, the desk quantifies the real conflict between banks and bondholders in cents per dollar of claim (+upside or -discount):

  • Bank Syndicate Cooperation vs DSRA Cash Trap (-4.5c to +2.0c): Senior secured project finance loans have 6-month Debt Service Reserve Accounts (DSRA) and minimum DSCR covenants (1.15x–1.20x). If banks turn hostile, they can trap all plant cash flows inside OpCo, starving HoldCo of coupon/redemption liquidity (-4.5c discount). If banks cooperate on a consensual A&E, cash flows upstream freely (+2.0c upside). Base Case reflects -1.5c friction.
  • Structural Priming & Creditor-on-Creditor Uptier Risk (-3.5c to +1.0c): Domestic Turkish banks holding bilateral working capital loans could demand first-lien pledges over unencumbered Zorlu Yenilenebilir (renewable subsidiary) or ZES (EV charging) shares as a condition of rolling bank lines, structurally priming HoldCo Eurobonds (-3.5c discount). Supranational DFI oversight (EBRD/IFC) prevents aggressive priming (+1.0c). Base Case reflects -0.5c.
  • Turkish Sovereign Macro & Lira FX Transmission (-4.0c to +2.0c): OEDAŞ grid tariffs and retail revenues are TRY-denominated and EMRA-regulated. Rapid Lira depreciation creates cash conversion lags and high CBRT interest rates compress HoldCo cash (-4.0c discount). Macro stabilization and disinflation yield +2.0c upside. Base Case reflects -1.0c.
  • Zorlu Yenilenebilir IPO & Strategic Monetization (-2.5c to +3.5c): Successful IPO of the renewable arm on Borsa Istanbul or a minority stake sale to a Gulf sovereign wealth fund (e.g., TAQA, Masdar) would inject $250M+ cash directly to retire 2026 Eurobonds (+3.5c upside).
