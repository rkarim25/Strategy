1. Why Express Qualitative Factors in Cents Rather Than %?

While Zorlu's fundamental unencumbered asset coverage is robust at 190.6% ($2,598M EV vs $1,363M net debt), secondary bonds trade at a discount (~94.5c) due to macro and execution frictions. Expressing qualitative adjustments directly in cents per dollar of claim (+upside or -discount) gives direct, actionable attribution: Desk Fair Value = Fundamental Senior Recovery + ∑(Δ Cents).

  • Turkish Sovereign Macro / Lira FX Beta (-5.0c to +2.0c): Severe Lira devaluation and high domestic CBRT rates compress cash conversion at the regulated grid (-5c discount). Disinflation and currency stabilization provide +2c upside.

  • Refinancing Execution & EM Illiquidity (-4.0c to 0.0c): Rollover friction on the $300M 2026 Eurobond maturity wall and CCC/B- dealer spread penalty (-4c to 0c discount).
