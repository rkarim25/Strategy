📉 4. Closed-Loop Bond Recovery & Valuation Engine: Exit Yield, Tenor Extension, Haircuts & Standstill Drag Bridging Theoretical Enterprise Value to Secondary Market Clearing Prices for All Traded Bonds
In an extrajudicial or Chapter 11 restructuring, senior creditors do not receive a single static cash recovery. Instead, consideration is paid in a package of reinstated exit notes and reorganized equity. The fair market value of that package depends on four dynamic, interacting variables: the Exit Yield ($Y_{\text{exit}}$), the Extension Tenor ($T_{\text{ext}}$), the Senior Principal Haircut ($H$) & Equitization ($E_{\text{swap}}$), and the Standstill Negotiation Duration ($T_{\text{nego}}$). Every shift in exit yield or restructuring delay directly reprices every bond in the capital structure.

⚙️ The Closed-Loop Mathematical Framework
1. Reinstated Exit Debt Pricing ($P_{\text{new}}$):
Pnew = ∑ [ (C/2) / (1 + Yexit/2)t ] + [ 100 / (1 + Yexit/2)2×Text ]

Exit debt issued with coupon $C$ below the market clearing yield $Y_{\text{exit}}$ trades at an immediate day-one discount. At Base ($C = 7.50\%$, $Y_{\text{exit}} = 9.75\%$, $T_{\text{ext}} = 8\text{Y}$), the new paper trades at 87.7c.

2. Plan Emergence Pot ($\text{Rec}_{\text{emergence}}$):
Recemergence = [ (1 - H) · (1 - Eswap) · Pnew ] + [ (1 - H) · Eswap · 100 · μeq ]

After a 35% haircut ($H = 0.35$) and 30% equitization ($E_{\text{swap}} = 0.30$), senior creditors receive 45.5c in reinstated debt (worth $45.5 \times 0.877 = 39.9\text{c}$) + 19.5c in reorganized equity = 59.4c total emergence pot.

3. Standstill Negotiation Time Drag ($\text{DF}_{\text{nego}}$):
DFnego = 1 / (1 + Yexit / 2)2 × (Tnego / 12)

Creditors suffer zero coupon flow during negotiations while legal fees bleed -$15M/month. A 12-month standstill discounts emergence proceeds by -9.08% ($\text{DF} = 0.9092$).

4. Present Economic Recovery ($\text{Rec}_{\text{PV}}$):
RecPV = Recemergence × DFnego = 59.4c × 0.9092 = 54.0c Net PV

This 54.0c true economic recovery serves as the core anchor. Individual bond prices are derived by applying tranche-specific indenture deltas.

📘 6. Qualitative Risk Assessment Demystified: Expressing Qualitative Factors in Cents of Upside or Discount to Recovery Replacing Abstract % Multipliers with Transparent Cents Attribution per Dollar of Claim
In classical distressed debt models, analysts often use abstract percentage multipliers (e.g. "25% friction" or "15% discount"). However, institutional credit traders and portfolio managers demand direct attribution: "How many cents per dollar does this specific risk take off my bond?" By expressing qualitative risk factors directly as Cents of Upside (+cents) or Discount (-cents) to Recovery, the bridge between financial models and market pricing becomes completely transparent: Desk Fair Value = Present Economic Recovery + Tranche Indenture Delta + ∑(Δ Cents).
