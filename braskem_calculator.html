1. Why Express Qualitative Factors in Cents Rather Than Abstract %?

In distressed credit trading, percentage multipliers obscure how much economic value is actually at stake. Expressing qualitative adjustments directly in cents per dollar of claim (+upside or -discount) gives direct attribution: Desk Fair Value = Present Economic Recovery + Indenture Delta + ∑(Δ Cents).

  • Petrobras Sponsor Governance (-6.0c to +4.0c): Refusal to fund Novonor forces heavy creditor equitization (-6c discount). A direct sponsor equity check or ADNOC buyout provides +4c upside.
  • Maceió Litigation Overhang (-6.0c to +1.0c): Rotterdam class action piercing provisions threatens priority cash sweeps (-6c discount). Final federal judicial release caps liabilities (+1c upside).
  • Judicial Resolution Pathway (-8.0c to +2.0c): Collapse into a 24-month in-court RJ burns $15M/mo in advisor fees and adds DIP super-priority (-8c discount). Fast-track consensual plan approval preserves +2c.
  • Secondary Market Liquidity (-5.0c to 0.0c): Mandate-driven forced-selling and wide dealer bid/ask spreads during restructuring impose a -5c to 0c illiquidity haircut.
2. How the Math Works: Direct Cents Addition to Present Value

Secondary bond fair value is governed by the closed-loop link between Exit Yield ($Y_{\text{exit}}$), Tenor ($T_{\text{ext}}$), Haircut ($H$), and Negotiation Duration ($T_{\text{nego}}$), adjusted directly by qualitative cents:

1. Exit Paper Price (Pnew): Pnew = ∑ [ (C/2) / (1 + Yexit/2)t ] + 100 / (1 + Yexit/2)2Text
2. Standstill Time Discount (DFnego): DFnego = 1 / (1 + Yexit/2)2 × (Tnego / 12)
3. Present Economic Recovery: RecPV = [ (1 - H) × (1 - Eswap) × Pnew + (1 - H) × Eswap × μeq ] × DFnego
4. Net Qualitative Delta (Δqual): Δqual = ΔPetrobras + ΔMaceió + ΔRJ + ΔLiquidity (Base = -4.0c)
5. Desk Fair Value: Fair Value = RecPV + Δtranche + Δqual (Base: 54.0c + 0c - 4.0c = 50.0c)
3. Why Are There 3 Sets of Prices (Floor, Fair, Upside) for Every Scenario?

A crucial distinction in institutional distressed credit is between Macro Fundamental Regimes (The Scenario Presets) and Secondary Market Trading Bands (Market Execution Dispersion):

• The Scenario Presets / Sliders (The Fundamental Reality): Sets the macro balance sheet parameters: Operating EBITDA ($1,400M Base), Multiple (5.0x), Haircut (35%), Equitization (30%), Exit Yield (9.75%), Standstill (18M), and Net Qualitative Friction (-4.0c).
• Trading Floor (Stress Exit / Secondary Bid): Even if the company achieves this scenario's restructuring terms, secondary market conditions may be hostile upon emergence. If exit yields blow out +200 bps (to 11.75%), dealer inventories are frozen, and mandate selling floods the street, secondary clearing prices fall to this liquidation floor (e.g. ~35c–40c for 2033s).
• Desk Fair Value (Central Model / Expected PV): The theoretical present value of the reinstated paper plus reorganized equity under the exact dialed-in inputs (e.g. 50.0c for the benchmark 2033s). This is your central trading anchor.
• Emergence Upside (Post-Reorg Re-Rating): Conversely, once the default cloud clears and leverage drops to <3.2x, the market often re-rates the company aggressively: exit yields compress -150 bps (to 8.25%), new equity trades at a premium public multiple (6.0x+), or a strategic sponsor tenders for paper, pulling the trading value toward par (e.g. ~65c–75c).
