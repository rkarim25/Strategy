2-Year Note (DV01: $19.2)

Sweet spot: Rapid Fed easing cycles, bear steepeners, safe-haven cash surrogate.

Direct proxy for near-term Fed funds rate. Shields against long-end duration bloodbaths.

5-Year Note (DV01: $46.5)

Sweet spot: Cycle turning points, curve steepeners, optimal carry & roll-down.

Captures ~90% of curve yield with only ~28% of 30Y duration volatility. Best risk-adjusted Sharpe ratio.

10-Year Note (DV01: $82.0)

Sweet spot: Disinflationary slowdowns, classic 60/40 equity drawdown hedging.

Global cost of capital anchor. Vulnerable when inflation or sovereign debt deficits become structural.

30-Year Bond (DV01: $165.0)

Sweet spot: Deflationary recessions and severe liquidity shocks (bull flatteners).

High convexity duration instrument. Severely penalized by supply indigestion and term premium repricing.
